1. ## Solarhub App Overview

   ## 1. Registration and Login

   For details, please refer to [Registration and Login]({{< ref "/login-register">}} "Registration and Login")

   ## 2. Creating a Device

   Newly registered accounts need to create a device first.

   <img src="/../../en/photo/docs/quickStart/add-device-app.png" style="margin-right: 50px" width="280">

   <img src="/../../en/photo/docs/quickStart/add-device-app2.png" style="margin-right: 50px" width="280">

   <img src="/../../en/photo/docs/quickStart/add-device-app3.png" style="margin-right: 50px" width="280">

   1. Click Create Device

   2. Add the device by scanning a QR code or manually entering the serial number.

   3. Enter the network configuration interface

   <img src="/../../en/photo/docs/quickStart/add-device-app4.png" style="margin-right: 50px" width="280">

   <img src="/../../en/photo/docs/quickStart/add-device-app5.png" style="margin-right: 50px" width="280">

   <img src="/../../en/photo/docs/quickStart/add-device-app6.png" style="margin-right: 50px" width="280">

   Enter the Wi-Fi password to configure the network. During configuration, please ensure that the device and the phone being configured are on the **same 2.4GHz** Wi-Fi network.

   **Note:** During operation, please allow the application to access Bluetooth, location, and data permissions, and enable Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and location services. Otherwise, the device may be **unselectable** or **network pairing may fail**.

   <img src="/../../en/photo/docs/quickStart/add-device-app7.png" style="margin-right: 50px" width="280">

   <img src="/../../en/photo/docs/quickStart/add-device-app8.png" style="margin-right: 50px" width="280">

   <img src="/../../en/photo/docs/quickStart/add-device-app9.png" style="margin-right: 50px" width="280">

   Normally, you should wait 10-30 seconds and then refresh to see the device data.

   ## 3. Device Parameter Settings

   <img src="/../../en/photo/docs/quickStart/device-set.png" style="margin-right: 50px" width="280">

   <img src="/../../en/photo/docs/quickStart/device-set3.png" style="margin-right: 50px" width="280">

   <img src="/../../en/photo/docs/quickStart/device-set4.png" style="margin-right: 50px;margin-top: 20px" width="280">

   <img src="/../../en/photo/docs/quickStart/device-set4.png" style="margin-right: 50px;margin-top: 20px" width="280">

   1. Access parameter settings on the device details page.

   2. Modify parameters and then apply the settings.

   3. Wait a few seconds for the setting results to be displayed.
