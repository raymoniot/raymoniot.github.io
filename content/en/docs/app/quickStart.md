## Solarhub App Overview

## 1. Registration and Login

For details, please refer to [Registration and Login]({{< ref "login-register" >}} "Registration and Login")

## 2. Creating a Device

Newly registered accounts need to create a device first.

<img src="../../../photo/docs/quickStart/add-device-app.png" style="margin-right: 50px" width="300">

<img src="../../../photo/docs/quickStart/add-device-app2.png" style="margin-right: 50px" width="300">

<img src="../../../photo/docs/quickStart/add-device-app3.png" style="margin-right: 50px" width="300">

1. Click Create Device
2. Add the device by scanning a QR code or entering the serial number manually
3. Enter the network configuration interface

<img src="../../../photo/docs/quickStart/add-device-app4.png" style="margin-right: 50px" width="300">

<img src="../../../photo/docs/quickStart/add-device-app5.png" style="margin-right: 50px" width="300">

<img src="../../../photo/docs/quickStart/add-device-app6.png" style="margin-right: 50px" width="300">

Enter the Wi-Fi password to configure the network. During configuration, please make sure the device and the configuring phone are on the **same 2.4GHz** Wi-Fi network.

**Note**: during the operation, please allow the application to access Bluetooth, location and data permissions, and enable Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and location services, otherwise the **device may not be selectable** or the **network configuration may fail**.

<img src="../../../photo/docs/quickStart/add-device-app7.png" style="margin-right: 50px" width="300">

<img src="../../../photo/docs/quickStart/add-device-app8.png" style="margin-right: 50px" width="300">

<img src="../../../photo/docs/quickStart/add-device-app9.png" style="margin-right: 50px" width="300">

Normally, wait 10–30 seconds and refresh to see the device data
