# Function Overview

Plant Layout is used to view the physical layout and connection relationships of the modules/devices in this plant, as well as the power curve corresponding to the layout.

## 1. Create Layout

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/app/plant/layout1.png" style="margin-right: 24px" width="300">

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/app/plant/layout3.png" style="margin-right: 0px" width="300">

### Steps

1. On the plant overview page, tap the "Layout" tab at the bottom
2. When the plant has no layout yet, the page shows a "Create Layout" button; after tapping it, the platform automatically generates the corresponding components based on the devices under the plant
3. After generation, you can drag components to adjust their positions, or tap "Edit" below to edit a single component (adjust its direction and angle). After editing, tap "Save" in the upper right corner

## 2. View Layout Data

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/app/plant/layout6.png" style="margin-right: 24px" width="300">

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/app/plant/layout9.png" style="margin-right: 0px" width="300">

### Steps

1. There is a "Show power curve" button at the bottom of the layout page; tap it to expand the power curve of the day
2. Tap a time point on the curve, and the data of each component in the layout switches synchronously to the power at that moment
3. Use the arrows on both sides of the date at the top to switch to the previous/next day; tap the date to select a date manually
4. The buttons in the lower right corner of the layout page are: Locate (reset the layout view), Refresh, and Edit (enter editing mode)

> Note: **creating and editing** a layout is more convenient on the plant details page of the Web platform (batch adding components, adjusting angles); the App is mainly used for viewing and on-site fine-tuning.
