# Function Overview

To view devices in the Plant User Perspective: select "Device List" in the menu in the upper right corner of the plant overview page.

## Difference from the Professional Consultant Perspective

- **Plant User Perspective**: the device list only shows the devices under the **current plant**, suitable for users who only view the devices of their own plant
- **Professional Consultant Perspective**: the "Device" tab at the bottom shows all devices, allowing filtering across plants by logger and device status, and searching by serial number

## Steps

1. On the plant overview page, tap the menu in the upper right corner and select "Device List"
2. The page shows the devices under the current plant (loggers and their sub-devices), which can be filtered by device type and status
3. Tap a device card to enter the details page of that device

> Note: if there are no devices under the plant yet, the page will show "No data"; in that case you need to bind a logger to the plant through "Add Logger".
