# Function Overview

Tap "Unbind Device" in the menu in the upper right corner of the device details page to remove the binding relationship between the device and the plant (or between a sub-device and the logger).

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/app/device/deviceview-unbind.png" style="margin-right: 50px" width="300">

## Steps

1. On the device details page, tap the menu in the upper right corner
2. Select "Unbind Device"
3. Confirm the prompt to complete unbinding

## Notes

- **Unbinding a sub-device deletes the sub-device from the logger and clears the data of the sub-device**; please operate with care
- After a logger is unbound, its binding relationship with the plant is removed, and it needs to be added again to continue reporting data
- Unbinding cannot be undone
