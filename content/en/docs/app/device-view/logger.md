# Function Overview

You can also add a logger directly in the Device User Perspective: the entry is in the menu in the upper right corner of the device details page.

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/app/view/plant-logger.webp" style="margin-right: 24px" width="300">

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/app/view/plant-logger2.webp" style="margin-right: 0px" width="300">

## Steps

1. On the device details page, tap the menu in the upper right corner and select "Add Logger"
2. Scan the logger QR code, or tap the keyboard icon to manually enter the logger serial number
3. After entering it, tap "Add Device"

> Note: this is **an entry unique to the Device User Perspective** (there is no Add Logger in the device details of the Professional Consultant Perspective); the logger must first be imported on the platform.
