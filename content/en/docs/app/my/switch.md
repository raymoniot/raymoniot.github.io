1. Select My in the menu bar at the bottom of the APP, and you can switch the access mode of the App in Switch perspective
2. There are two access perspectives, professional consultant and end user
- Professional consultant: Use the APP as an equipment manufacturer or dealer, focusing on all power stations and equipment under the platform
- End user: Use the APP as a power station owner, focusing on my power station and equipment
