## Overview of Plant

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/hess-overview.png">

The overview of the plant for HESS includes four sections: power generation, power grid, battery, and power consumption.

- **Power generation**: Display the power generation power, photovoltaic installed capacity, daily power generation, monthly power generation, annual power generation, and annual power generation of HESS plants under the data permission of the current logged-in user
- **Grid**: Display the purchase and sale power of HESS plants under the data permission of the current logged-in user
- **Battery**: Display the SOC, daily discharge, and daily charging of HESS plants under the data permission of the current logged-in user
- **Power consumption**: Display the power consumption power, daily power consumption, monthly power consumption, annual power consumption, and total power consumption of HESS plants under the data permission of the current logged-in user

## Plant details page

By clicking on the plant in the plant list, you can view the details page of a single plant. The details page displays detailed information of the plant, including plant chart, plant equipment, plant alarm, plant layout, plant information, etc.

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/plant-detail-hess.png">

### Function overview

- **Plant chart view**: You can view the total statistical information of the plant based on the equipment summary for the day, month and year. Different statistical periods display different parameter information. It supports the parameter selection of power generation power, power consumption power, battery remaining capacity, grid power, and battery power. Multiple parameters can be displayed intuitively in the chart.
- **Daily view**: Display the selected parameter trend chart through parameter selection
- **Weekly view**: Display the power consumption, daily charging and daily discharge trends of the plant in a week
- **Monthly view**: Display the power generation, daily charging, daily discharge, purchase and sale trends of the plant every day in a month
- **Yearly view**: Display the power generation, daily charging, daily discharge, purchase and sale trends of the plant in each month of a year
- **Total view**: Display the power generation, daily charging, daily discharge, purchase and sale trends of the plant every year
- Other contents are the same as those of the photovoltaic system, please refer to [Function Overview]({{< ref "/pvs#Function Overview" >}} "Photovoltaic System")
