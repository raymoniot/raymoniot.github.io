# Function Overview

The Residential Storage page is used to manage plants whose business type is Residential Storage. This type of plant contains both photovoltaic power generation and battery storage, so the overview and charts display the data of power generation, power consumption, power grid and battery at the same time.

## Plant Overview

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/hess-overview.webp">

The plant overview of the Residential Storage includes four sections: power generation, power grid, battery, and power consumption.

- **Power generation**: Summary display of the power generation power, PV Capacity, daily power generation, monthly power generation, annual power generation, and annual power generation of Residential Storage plants under the data permissions of the current logged-in user
- **Power grid**: Display the purchase and sale power summary of Residential Storage plants under the data permissions of the current logged-in user
- **Battery**: Display the SOC, daily discharge and daily charge summary of Residential Storage plants under the data permissions of the current logged-in user
- **Power consumption**: Display the power consumption power, daily power consumption, monthly power consumption, annual power consumption, and total power consumption of Residential Storage plants under the data permissions of the current logged-in user

## Plant details page

By clicking on a plant in the plant list, you can view the details page of a single plant. The details page displays the detailed information of the plant, including plant charts, plant devices, plant alarms, plant layout, plant info, etc.

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/plant-detail-hess.webp">

The top of the page displays the plant name, location, owner and the latest Data Update Time. The types of operations in the upper right corner are the same as the PVS (Add Collector, System large screen, Create work order, Power Generation Repair, Layout editing, etc.), for details please refer to [Plant details page]({{< ref "/pvs#plant-details-page" >}} "Plant details page").

### Function Overview

- **Plant chart view**: You can view the total statistical information of the plant based on the device summary for the day, month and year. Different statistical periods display different parameter information. It supports parameter selection of power generation power, power consumption power, battery remaining capacity, grid power, and battery power, and multiple parameters can be displayed intuitively in the chart.

  - Daily view: Display the selected parameter trend chart through parameter selection
  - Weekly view: Display the power consumption power, daily charge and daily discharge trends of the plant in a week
  - Monthly view: Display the power generation, daily charge, daily discharge, purchase and sale trends of the plant every day in a certain month
  - Yearly view: Display the power generation, daily charge, daily discharge, purchase and sale trends of the plant every month in a certain year
  - Total view: Display the power generation, daily charge, daily discharge, purchase and sale trends of the plant every year

- Other contents are the same as the photovoltaic system, for details please refer to [Function Overview]({{< ref "/pvs#function-overview" >}} "PVS")
