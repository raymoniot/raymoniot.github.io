# Function Overview

The Battery System page is used to manage plants whose business type is Battery System. Its page structure is the same as the PVS (overview, list, map, System large screen, details page); the difference is that the overview metrics and the details page charts summarize charge and discharge data.

## Plant Overview

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/bess-overview.webp">

The plant overview of the Battery System includes four sections: real-time power, Charge/Discharge Statistics, social benefits, and online plants.

- **Real-time power**: Summary display of the Battery System power and installed capacity under the data permissions of the current logged-in user in the Battery System
- **Charge/Discharge Statistics**: Display the daily charge, daily discharge, cumulative charge, and cumulative discharge of the PVS plant under the data permissions of the current logged-in user
- **Social benefits**: The social benefits generated by the entire PVS under the data permissions of the current logged-in user, including tree species index, carbon dioxide emission reduction, and light bulb usage
- **Online plants**: The number of online plants and the total number of plants under the data permissions of the current logged-in user

## Plant details page

By clicking on a plant in the plant list, you can view the details page of a single plant. The details page displays the detailed information of the plant, including plant charts, plant devices, plant alarms, plant layout, plant info, etc.

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/plant-detail-bess.webp">

The top of the page displays the plant name, location, owner and the latest Data Update Time. The types of operations in the upper right corner are the same as the PVS (Add Collector, System large screen, Create work order, Power Generation Repair, Layout editing, etc.), for details please refer to [Plant details page]({{< ref "/pvs#plant-details-page" >}} "Plant details page").

### Function Overview

- **Plant chart view**: You can view the total statistical information of the plant based on the device summary for the day, month and year. Different statistical periods display different parameter information. It supports parameter selection of SOC, SOH, daily charge, daily discharge, cumulative charge, cumulative discharge, battery temperature, total current, total voltage, and total power, and multiple parameters can be displayed intuitively in the chart.

  - Daily view: Display the selected parameter trend chart through parameter selection
  - Weekly view: Display the daily charge and daily discharge trend chart of the plant in a week
  - Monthly view: Display the daily charge, daily discharge and revenue summary of each day in a certain month of the plant
  - Yearly view: Display the charge, discharge and revenue summary of each month in a certain year of the plant
  - Total view: Display the charge, discharge and revenue summary of each year of the plant

- Other contents are the same as the photovoltaic system, for details please refer to [Function Overview]({{< ref "/pvs#function-overview" >}} "PVS")
