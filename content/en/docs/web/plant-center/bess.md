## Overview of Plant

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/bess-overview.png">

The overview of the plant of the BESS includes four sections: real-time power, charge and discharge statistics, social benefits, and online plant.

- **Real-time power**: Summary display of BESS power and installed capacity under the data permission of the current logged-in user in the BESS
- **Charge and discharge statistics**: Display the daily charge, daily discharge, cumulative charge, and cumulative discharge of the photovoltaic plant under the data permission of the current logged-in user
- **Social benefits**: The social benefits generated by the entire photovoltaic system under the data permission of the current logged-in user, including tree species index, carbon dioxide emission reduction, and light bulb usage
- **Online plant**: The number of online plants and the total number of plants under the data permission of the current logged-in user

## Plant details page

By clicking on a plant in the plant list, you can view the details page of a single plant. The details page displays detailed information about the plant, including plant charts, plant equipment, plant alarms, plant layout, plant information, etc.

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/plant-detail-bess.png">

### Function Overview

- **Plant chart view**: You can view the total statistical information of the plant based on the equipment summary for the day, month and year. Different statistical periods display different parameter information. It supports parameter selection of SOC, SOH, daily charge, daily discharge, cumulative charge, cumulative discharge, battery temperature, total current, total voltage, and total power. Multiple parameters can be displayed intuitively in the chart.

- Daily view: Display the selected parameter trend chart through parameter selection
- Weekly view: Display the daily charge and discharge trend chart of the plant in a week
- Monthly view: Display the daily charge, discharge and revenue summary of the plant every day in a month
- Yearly view: Display the charge, discharge and revenue summary of the plant every month in a year
- Total view: Display the charge, discharge and revenue summary of the plant every year

- Other contents are the same as the photovoltaic system, please refer to [Function Overview]({{< ref "/pvs#Function Overview" >}} "Photovoltaic System")
