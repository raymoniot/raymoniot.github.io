## Overview of Power Plant

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/bess-overview.png">

The overview of the power plant of the battery system includes four sections: real-time power, charge and discharge statistics, social benefits, and online power plant.

- **Real-time power**: Summary display of battery system power and installed capacity under the data permission of the current logged-in user in the battery system
- **Charge and discharge statistics**: Display the daily charge, daily discharge, cumulative charge, and cumulative discharge of the photovoltaic power station under the data permission of the current logged-in user
- **Social benefits**: The social benefits generated by the entire photovoltaic system under the data permission of the current logged-in user, including tree species index, carbon dioxide emission reduction, and light bulb usage
- **Online power station**: The number of online power stations and the total number of power stations under the data permission of the current logged-in user

## Power station details page

By clicking on a power station in the power station list, you can view the details page of a single power station. The details page displays detailed information about the power station, including power station charts, power station equipment, power station alarms, power station layout, power station information, etc.

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/plant-detail-bess.png">

### Function Overview

- **Power station chart view**: You can view the total statistical information of the power station based on the equipment summary for the day, month and year. Different statistical periods display different parameter information. It supports parameter selection of SOC, SOH, daily charge, daily discharge, cumulative charge, cumulative discharge, battery temperature, total current, total voltage, and total power. Multiple parameters can be displayed intuitively in the chart.

- Daily view: Display the selected parameter trend chart through parameter selection
- Weekly view: Display the daily charge and discharge trend chart of the power station in a week
- Monthly view: Display the daily charge, discharge and revenue summary of the power station every day in a month
- Yearly view: Display the charge, discharge and revenue summary of the power station every month in a year
- Total view: Display the charge, discharge and revenue summary of the power station every year

- Other contents are the same as the photovoltaic system, please refer to [Function Overview]({{< ref "/pvs#Function Overview" >}} "Photovoltaic System")
