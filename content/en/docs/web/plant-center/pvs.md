## Overview of plant

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/psv-overview.png">

The overview of the PVS plant includes four sections: real-time power, daily power, social benefits, and online plant.

- **Real-time power**: Summary display of the power and installed capacity of photovoltaic plants under the current login user data permission in the PVS
- **Daily power**: Display the daily power, monthly power generation, annual power generation, and cumulative power generation of photovoltaic plants under the current login user data permission
- **Social benefits**: The social benefits generated by the entire PVS under the current login user data permission, including tree species index, carbon dioxide emission reduction, and light bulb usage
- **Online plants**: The number of online plants and the total number of plants under the current login user data permission

## Plant list

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/plant-list.png">

The plant list displays some information of all online, faulty, and offline plants. You can intuitively see the basic information of the plant, the online status of all plants, and you can also modify and delete the plant

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/plant-list2.png">

You can also use conditional search to find plants that meet the conditions

## Plant map

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/plant-map.png">

Intuitively display the online status and distribution of all plants. You can find qualified plants by plant name/address/owner

## System large screen

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/dashboard-enter.png">

The entrance to the system large screen is in the upper right corner of the plant overview module of each system in the plant center

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/dashboard.png">

The plant large screen is used to intuitively display the operating status of the entire system, including plant overview, real-time power, daily full power generation hour ranking, monthly power generation, annual power generation, social contribution and other information

## Plant details page

By clicking on the plant in the plant list, you can view the details page of a single plant. The details page shows the detailed information of the plant, including plant diagrams, plant equipment, plant alarms, plant layout, plant information, etc.

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/plant-detail.png">

### Function Overview

- **View and export plant charts**: You can view the total statistical information of the plant based on the device for the day, month and year. Different statistical periods display different parameter information. You can also export the statistical data in the chart into an Excel chart

- Daily view: Display the real-time power trend chart of a certain day

- Weekly view: Display the real-time power trend chart of a week

- Monthly view: Display the power generation and revenue summary of each day in a certain month

- Yearly view: Display the power generation and revenue summary of each month in a certain year
- Total view: Display the power generation and revenue summary of each year

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/plant-chart.png">

- **Plant large screen**: Display the overview information and power generation status, alarm information, etc. of the plant

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/plant-dashboard.png">

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/plant-dashboard2.png">

- **Add collector**: Bind a collector to the current plant. There are two ways to add a collector. For details, please refer to [Add collector]({{< ref "/add-logger" >}} "Add collector")

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/add-collect.png">

- **Create a work order**: Create a work order for this plant. For details, please refer to [Work order system]({{< ref "../ops/ticket/dashboard/" >}} "PVS")

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/ticket.png">

- **Power generation repair**: Manually modify the power generation value in case of incorrect power generation

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/power-fix.png">

- **Device list**: Displays all devices under the plant, and displays each device list according to the device classification

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/device-list.png">

- **Alarm list**: Displays the events that are being alarmed in the past 3 days, and can be filtered by advanced search conditions

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/alert.png">

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/alert2.png">

- **Layout**: Set the physical layout of the roof and the system layout of the component equipment connection

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/layout.png">

- Timeline drag: Support dragging the timeline to obtain photovoltaic panel information at different times

- Layout editing: Add photovoltaic panels by dragging components, support starting/disabling and deleting photovoltaic panels

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/layout-edit.png">

- **Information**: Detailed information display of plant

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/info.png">
