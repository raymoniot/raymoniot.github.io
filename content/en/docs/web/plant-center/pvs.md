## Overview of power station

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/psv-overview.png">

The overview of the photovoltaic system power station includes four sections: real-time power, daily power, social benefits, and online power station.

- **Real-time power**: Summary display of the power and installed capacity of photovoltaic power stations under the current login user data permission in the photovoltaic system
- **Daily power**: Display the daily power, monthly power generation, annual power generation, and cumulative power generation of photovoltaic power stations under the current login user data permission
- **Social benefits**: The social benefits generated by the entire photovoltaic system under the current login user data permission, including tree species index, carbon dioxide emission reduction, and light bulb usage
- **Online power stations**: The number of online power stations and the total number of power stations under the current login user data permission

## Power station list

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/plant-list.png">

The power station list displays some information of all online, faulty, and offline power stations. You can intuitively see the basic information of the power station, the online status of all power stations, and you can also modify and delete the power station

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/plant-list2.png">

You can also use conditional search to find power stations that meet the conditions

## Power station map

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/plant-map.png">

Intuitively display the online status and distribution of all power stations. You can find qualified power stations by power station name/address/owner

## System large screen

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/dashboard-enter.png">

The entrance to the system large screen is in the upper right corner of the power station overview module of each system in the power station center

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/dashboard.png">

The power station large screen is used to intuitively display the operating status of the entire system, including power station overview, real-time power, daily full power generation hour ranking, monthly power generation, annual power generation, social contribution and other information

## Power station details page

By clicking on the power station in the power station list, you can view the details page of a single power station. The details page shows the detailed information of the power station, including power station diagrams, power station equipment, power station alarms, power station layout, power station information, etc.

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/plant-detail.png">

### Function Overview

- **View and export power station charts**: You can view the total statistical information of the power station based on the device for the day, month and year. Different statistical periods display different parameter information. You can also export the statistical data in the chart into an Excel chart

- Daily view: Display the real-time power trend chart of a certain day

- Weekly view: Display the real-time power trend chart of a week

- Monthly view: Display the power generation and revenue summary of each day in a certain month

- Yearly view: Display the power generation and revenue summary of each month in a certain year
- Total view: Display the power generation and revenue summary of each year

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/plant-chart.png">

- **Power station large screen**: Display the overview information and power generation status, alarm information, etc. of the power station

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/plant-dashboard.png">

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/plant-dashboard2.png">

- **Add collector**: Bind a collector to the current power station. There are two ways to add a collector. For details, please refer to [Add collector]({{< ref "/add-logger" >}} "Add collector")

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/add-collect.png">

- **Create a work order**: Create a work order for this power station. For details, please refer to [Work order system]({{< ref "../ops/ticket/dashboard/" >}} "Photovoltaic system")

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/ticket.png">

- **Power generation repair**: Manually modify the power generation value in case of incorrect power generation

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/power-fix.png">

- **Device list**: Displays all devices under the power station, and displays each device list according to the device classification

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/device-list.png">

- **Alarm list**: Displays the events that are being alarmed in the past 3 days, and can be filtered by advanced search conditions

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/alert.png">

- **Layout**: Set the physical layout of the roof and the system layout of the component equipment connection

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/layout.png">

- Timeline drag: Support dragging the timeline to obtain photovoltaic panel information at different times

- Layout editing: Add photovoltaic panels by dragging components, support starting/disabling and deleting photovoltaic panels

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/plant/layout-edit.png">

- **Information**: Detailed information display of power station
