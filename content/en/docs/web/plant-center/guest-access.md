# Function Overview

Visitor Authorization grants the viewing permission of a plant to other accounts on the platform. The authorized account (visitor) can see this plant in its own Plant List and view its data, but cannot modify or delete the plant — management operations such as modifying, deleting and adding a collector can still only be performed by the plant owner.

Multiple visitors can be added to one plant; deleting a visitor revokes that account's viewing permission.

## 1. Authorization Entry

In [Modify Plant]({{< ref "/edit-plant" >}} "Modify Plant"), authorize through [Add Stakeholders]({{< ref "create-plant/#23-add-stakeholders-visitors" >}} "Modify Plant") in Complete the Plant Information

Enter the email address of the platform account in the "Plant Visitor" row and click add, and that account gains the viewing permission of this plant; you can also add it directly in step 3 "Stakeholders" when creating a plant.
