# Function Overview

Modifying a plant is used to update the plant's basic information, location, stakeholders and photos. Errors made when creating the plant, or changes that occur later (for example, a changed contact, or the need to authorize a new visitor), are all adjusted here.

## 1. Modify Plant Entry

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/edit-plant/edit-plant.png">

Click "Modify" in the operation column on the right side of the Plant List (you can also enter from the plant details page)

<img src="../../../../photo/docs/edit-plant/edit-plant1.png">

For the steps to modify a plant, please refer to [Complete the Plant Information]({{< ref "/create-plant/#2-complete-the-plant-information" >}} "Create Plant")

Please note:

- **Business Type** cannot be modified; once the plant is created, the business type is fixed
- Only the plant owner (and organization administrators with the corresponding permissions) can modify or delete the plant; accounts authorized as visitors can only view
- Adding a visitor email in "Stakeholders" authorizes the plant to other accounts, with the same effect as [Visitor Authorization]({{< ref "/guest-access" >}} "Visitor Authorization")
