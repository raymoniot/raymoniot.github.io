## Solarhub Web Overview

Solarhub Web provides comprehensive energy monitoring services such as photovoltaic, wind power, and energy storage, and provides professional and efficient comprehensive energy cloud monitoring platform services for medium and large power stations, equipment manufacturers, operation and maintenance providers, and household power station owners.

## Have a Solarhub account

<img src="../../../photo/docs/overview.png">

- Experience account: In order to facilitate you to quickly experience the functions of Solarhub, we have opened a customer experience account. You can communicate with the business docking person in charge and enter the experience account and password to experience Solarhub. **Currently supports mainstream browsers, it is recommended that you use Chrome58, Firefox49, IE9 and above**.

Operation steps: Visit Solarhub (Web), select "Login method", and enter the experience account and password to log in to Solarhub.
