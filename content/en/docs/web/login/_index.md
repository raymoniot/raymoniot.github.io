# Function Overview

This section explains how to log in to Solarhub (Web):

- [Login]({{< ref "login" >}} "Login"): login entry, account and password, App download and multi-language switch
- [Multi-language Switch]({{< ref "languages" >}} "Multi-language Switch"): how to switch the interface language before and after login

Accounts are created by the platform provider or by the administrator of your organization. If you forget the password, contact the administrator of your organization to reset it in "Organization management → Organizational Structure".
