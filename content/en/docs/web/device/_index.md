# Function Overview

The Device Center is divided by device type into pages such as Collector, Inverter, Battery System, Optimizer and Smart Meter. On each page you can view all devices of that type, filter and search by status, and enter a single device's details page to view information and historical data.

Device data is reported by the collector: on-site inverters, batteries, meters, optimizers and so on hang under the collector as **sub-devices**, and the collector collects and reports their data to the platform in a unified way. Therefore, in addition to the device's own information, the device list also shows the **plant** and the **collector** it belongs to; click either to jump to it.

Common operations of the device list and the details page:

- **List**: the status tabs show the number of all, online, faulty and offline devices, and clicking a tab filters them; the search box searches by serial number, alias and other keywords; click the serial number to enter the device details page
- **Details page**: the top shows the device name, the latest data update time (including timezone), and the favorite and refresh buttons; the upper right corner has "Alarm" and "Unbind", the other operations are in the "…" menu; below are the device information cards and historical data charts
