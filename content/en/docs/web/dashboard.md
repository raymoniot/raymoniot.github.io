# Function Overview

The data dashboard is the default home page after logging in. It shows the plant situation within the data permissions of the current account from a macro point of view: the total number of plants, the number of plants of each type, and the geographic distribution of the plants.

There are also two shortcuts in the upper right corner of the page:

- **My work orders**: go to the [Work Order List]({{< ref "ops/ticket/list" >}} "Work Order List") to see the work orders related to you
- **My favorites**: quickly open the plants you follow (click the star in front of the plant name in the plant list to follow a plant)

## Business use

- As an O&M provider or installer, you need to know the category and number of plants connected to your company and the device distribution. This page shows the total number of plants and the number of plants of each type on the platform.
